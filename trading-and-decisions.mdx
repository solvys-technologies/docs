# Trading and decisions

Fintheon links market intelligence to a deliberate decision workflow. It keeps research, proposals, account capability, and execution-adjacent tools connected without representing every surface as a live broker.

## Decision work

Desk Plans, trade proposals, trade ideas, pre-trade reasoning, and the decision layer give a trader a place to document what they saw, what they expected, and what would change the thesis.

## Trading connections

Fintheon can work with supported provider-connected views and trading settings. Availability depends on a connected provider, account state, permissions, and the device in use.

## Safety and clarity

A provider connection governs what is actually available. Fintheon should label unavailable data or execution capability honestly, and it should never imply that a research screen, proposal, or scenario has placed a trade.

<Warning>
  Market data, analysis, and prior results do not guarantee future performance.
</Warning>
