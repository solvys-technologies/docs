# NarrativeFlow

NarrativeFlow tracks catalysts and market stories across time. It helps you distinguish a durable theme from a one-off headline and see when a narrative is strengthening, fading, or changing form.

## Inside a narrative session

A session can hold catalysts, evidence, linked instruments, notes, thesis changes, forecast scenarios, and saved history. Timeline, map, and grid views give different ways to inspect the same story.

## How it works with RiskFlow

RiskFlow finds and scores discrete events. NarrativeFlow connects the events that belong together, preserves their sequence, and makes the evidence trail reviewable.

## Best use

Create or open a session when several catalysts point to one market question. Add evidence deliberately, record competing explanations, and save the session so the next decision starts from context rather than memory.
