# Performance

Performance is Fintheon's long-horizon review system. It brings trade history, calendar context, decision notes, and behavioral signals into one place so a trader can evaluate process as well as results.

## What you can review

- Day and week summaries.
- Equity-curve and P&L views with high, low, and hover detail.
- Heatmaps for patterns across dates and sessions.
- Trading Calendar history and trade-level records.
- Trade annotations and linked decision context.
- PsychAssist daily rows and readiness signals.
- A compact daily performance rail for in-the-moment review.

## Product boundary

The Performance Journal owns durable review. Lightweight history, balance, and annotation handoffs may appear in other workspaces, but they link back to the journal instead of duplicating records.

## A practical review habit

At the end of a session, inspect the trade record, attach the relevant decision context, note what changed between plan and execution, then compare the result with your behavioral and market conditions. This helps separate a good process from a lucky outcome.

<Note>
  Where a connected provider or record is unavailable, Fintheon should show that state clearly rather than inventing results.
</Note>
