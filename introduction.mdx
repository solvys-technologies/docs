Fintheon is a desktop trading workspace built specifically for futures traders. Rather than juggling a news terminal, a chart platform, and a research tool separately, Fintheon brings all of them together: a live AI-scored news feed, a five-seat deliberation engine, an always-on analyst chat, and a narrative workspace where you can map how market themes connect. Sign in with your Google account and it's ready — no API keys, no manual configuration.

## What you can do with Fintheon

**Harper — your Chief Analyst Officer.** Harper is an AI analyst available 24/7 in the Consilium workspace. Ask it to summarize overnight developments, explain a move in crude or equity index futures, or stress-test a trade thesis. Harper has access to your RiskFlow feed and your daily briefs, so its answers are grounded in current market context, not generic financial knowledge.

**RiskFlow scored news.** Every news item that enters Fintheon is scored by an implied-volatility-weighted algorithm before it appears in your feed. Items with high IV scores surface first. The feed covers macro headlines, Fed speakers, earnings, and event-driven catalysts — all ranked by how much they are likely to move the instruments you trade.

**Arbitrum deliberation.** When you need a second opinion before a major decision, Arbitrum runs a five-seat AI chamber debate. The five seats argue the bull case, bear case, risk, quantitative, and macro perspectives and return a consensus probability, confidence level, dissent summary, and catalyst outlook. No trade tickets are generated — Arbitrum informs the decision; you make it.

**Sanctum narrative workspace.** Sanctum gives you a canvas to track the market narratives unfolding around your instruments. Catalyst cards promoted from RiskFlow appear here, and the Aquarium panel shows you the latest Arbitrum output in context. Your Morning, Afternoon, and Post-Market Daily Briefs all land in Sanctum as well.

**Daily briefs.** Four structured AI briefs publish automatically each trading day: the Morning Daily Brief (MDB) at 6:30 AM ET, the Afternoon Daily Brief (ADB) at 10:45 AM ET, the Post-Market Daily Brief (PMDB) at 5:15 PM ET, and the Tribune Weekly (TWT) on Sundays at 4:30 PM ET.

**Strategium mission control.** The right panel of the workspace shows your economic calendar, RiskFlow feed highlights, and platform status at a glance, so you always know where volatility is building without switching screens.

## Who Fintheon is for

Fintheon is for active futures traders who make discretionary decisions but want AI-level research quality at their fingertips. It is particularly useful if you trade instruments with strong narrative-driven moves — equity index futures, energy, metals, or interest rate futures — where context and speed matter as much as technical levels.
