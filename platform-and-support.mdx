# Platform and support

Fintheon includes operational controls for authorized users and support workflows.

## Platform controls

- Diagnostics and system status.
- Integrations and MCP connections.
- Usage, versioning, and desktop update controls.
- Support and administration tools.

These surfaces are account- and role-aware. Operator controls remain separate from a trader's normal research and decision environment.
