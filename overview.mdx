# Fintheon overview

Fintheon is a market-intelligence and decision-support workspace for traders. It connects live risk context, evolving narratives, research, decision work, trading tools, and performance review in one operating environment.

## The operating loop

1. **See what changed.** RiskFlow, the market calendar, and watchlists surface timely conditions and events.
2. **Make sense of it.** NarrativeFlow, Sanctum, CAO, and Arbitrum turn evidence into a reviewable thesis.
3. **Prepare the decision.** Desk Plans, proposals, and linked research keep the decision path explicit.
4. **Review the outcome.** Performance, journal records, and PsychAssist connect results back to process.

Fintheon supports judgment. It does not guarantee outcomes or place trades on a user's behalf.

## Product areas

- **Market intelligence:** RiskFlow, NarrativeFlow, economic intelligence, market data, and research.
- **Decision work:** Desk, Sanctum, Arbitrum, proposals, briefs, and QuantComposer.
- **Trading tools:** provider-connected trading views, settings, lockouts, and account-aware controls.
- **Performance:** journal, calendar, equity curve, heatmaps, trade history, annotations, and behavioral review.
- **Team and agents:** CAO, Forum, ProxVoice, Scouts, Autopilot, and governed workspaces.
