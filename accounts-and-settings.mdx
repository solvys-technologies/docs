# Accounts and settings

Settings brings personal preferences, connected providers, workspace access, and product controls into one place.

## What you manage

- Profile, preferences, and onboarding.
- Theme and desktop layout preferences.
- Connected accounts and supported provider controls.
- Notifications, availability, and device-specific permissions.
- Billing, plan access, and entitlements.
- Privacy, workspace membership, and support settings.

## Why it matters

Fintheon adapts to the account and device that are actually connected. A visible product area may still be limited by role, plan, provider capability, or a required setup step. Settings makes those boundaries inspectable and manageable.
