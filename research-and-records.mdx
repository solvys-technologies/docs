# Research and records

Fintheon keeps durable research separate from fast-moving market views. You can preserve a traceable snapshot of the evidence and return to it later.

## Included surfaces

- Memorandums and briefs.
- Quant Pill drafting and QuantComposer.
- FileRoom, documents, bookmarks, articles, and lexicon.
- Memory, context bank, context mentions, and research workspaces.

A saved record should keep its source reference and relevant context. This makes a later review possible without treating a transient screen as durable research.
