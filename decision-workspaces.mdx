# Decision workspaces

Fintheon provides several analytical workspaces that have clear jobs and handoffs.

## Desk

Desk is the preparation and decision surface. It includes Desk Plans, daily briefings, risk windows, calendar, inbox, screeners, decision telemetry, and connected context.

## Sanctum and Arbitrum

Sanctum holds timeline, chart context, risk assessment, theses, and catalyst management. Arbitrum preserves competing views, scenario branches, consensus, dissent, and reviewable verdicts.

## QuantComposer

QuantComposer helps draft and refine structured market reasoning from selected context. It belongs in the decision workflow, where its work can be inspected before it becomes a record or proposal.
