# Team and collaboration

Fintheon supports shared work without making private context public by default.

## Team surfaces

- Forum for shared discussion and attachments.
- ProxVoice for the live voice floor.
- Boardroom and War Room for structured team work.
- Desk membership, invitations, peers, and workspace-aware access.

Room membership, invite rules, and attachment access govern what is visible. Team features should preserve those rules when context moves between a chat, a room, a Desk, or a saved record.
