Apparatus is where the five Fintheon AI agents live. Open it from the **Apparatus** dropdown in the Consilium tab bar. The **Desk** sub-view shows a grid of intelligence briefing cards — one per agent — with each agent's current active narratives, memory facts, inter-agent connections, and conflict flags. Expanding a card reveals the full dossier.

Apparatus is not just an information display. Understanding how each agent thinks, what narratives they are tracking, and where they conflict helps you get better answers from the Agentic Forum and interpret the chamber verdicts in Sanctum's Aquarium.

## The five agents

## Agent protocol

The five agents follow a division of labor that Harper coordinates:

> **Harper orchestrates. Oracle analyzes. Feucht guards. Consul validates. Herald communicates.**

In practice this means: trade ideas flow from any agent through Harper's gatekeeper review before they surface as proposals. Oracle supplies the probabilistic framework. Feucht stress-tests with risk and level analysis. Consul checks the fundamental validity. Herald flags any sentiment divergence that could invalidate the thesis.

## The connection map

Expanding any agent card on the Desk shows its **Connections** section — a list of the active context channels between that agent and others. When you expand a card, animated lines draw between that agent's card and its connected peers, visually tracing the flow of information.

Key connections:

| Connection | What flows |
|---|---|
| Harper → Oracle | Kalshi position approvals, prediction market oversight |
| Harper → Feucht | Trade idea validation, risk limit enforcement |
| Harper → Consul | Fundamental thesis review, earnings calendar |
| Harper → Herald | Sentiment alerts, daily brief inputs |
| Oracle ↔ Consul | Macro overlay: prediction markets informed by fundamental analysis |
| Feucht ↔ Consul | Earnings catalysts informing futures positioning |
| Oracle ↔ Feucht | **Active conflict** — Oracle sees NQ upside from prediction model; Feucht flags elevated risk from VIX structure and morning flush patterns |

Conflict badges appear in the Apparatus header when agents hold directly opposed stances on the same narrative. A conflict is not an error — it is useful signal. The disagreement between Oracle and Feucht on NQ direction has been one of the most productive tensions on the platform.

## How agents collaborate in the Boardroom

When you submit a query in the Agentic Forum (Boardroom → Agentic Forum), all five agents receive the question simultaneously and respond in parallel during Wave 1 (Analysis). In Wave 2 (Deliberation), agents with connected context channels cross-check each other's output. Harper synthesizes in Wave 3, drawing on all four specialist responses plus any conflict signals flagged by the connection map.

<Tip>
  For the best Agentic Forum results, frame your question the way you would ask a trading desk: include the instrument, the timeframe, and what decision you are trying to make. The agents will calibrate their responses to your specific context.
</Tip>

The Apparatus Desk gives you the background to interpret those responses — knowing that Oracle is 72% on SPX upside while Feucht is hedging with put spreads tells you more about the deliberation verdict than the consensus probability alone.
