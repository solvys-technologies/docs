# PsychAssist

PsychAssist is Fintheon's readiness and behavioral review layer. It helps a trader recognize conditions that can affect decision quality before and during a session.

## Capabilities

- Tilt and emotional-regulation signals.
- Morning routine and setup-flow support.
- Hot-hand and readiness cues.
- Heartline, wearable, and sound-bridge settings where enabled.
- Daily journal rows linked to performance review.
- Trading lockout settings where an account or product policy supports them.

PsychAssist advises and records context. It does not replace a trader's judgment, a broker's controls, or professional care.
