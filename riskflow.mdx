# RiskFlow

RiskFlow is Fintheon's always-on risk and volatility intelligence surface. It turns headlines and events into scored, instrument-aware market context.

## What it does

RiskFlow evaluates incoming items for urgency, asymmetry, regime pressure, headwinds, tailwinds, likely market impact, and implied-volatility relevance. It gives a trader a clear reason to inspect an event instead of reacting to raw noise.

## Core capabilities

- A scored real-time feed with source and filter controls.
- IV scoring and event fuses for high-attention conditions.
- Bookmarks, visual enrichment, and market-awareness dossiers.
- Situation maps that connect a risk event to country, instrument, and media context.
- Economic reactions, prior prints, and event history.
- Notification metadata that respects device and preference settings.

## How to use it

Start with the most relevant instruments and filters, open a scored item, inspect its evidence and context, then save or send the item into a NarrativeFlow session, Desk plan, or research record when it matters.

<Warning>
  RiskFlow is decision support. A score is not investment advice, a trade signal, or a guarantee of market movement.
</Warning>
