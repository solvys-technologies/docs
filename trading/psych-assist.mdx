PsychAssist monitors your emotional state as you trade and surfaces warnings when your behavior patterns suggest elevated risk of an impulse mistake. It does not block your trading — it gives you information so you can decide. Think of it as a second pair of eyes that watches your recent session behavior while your attention is on the market.

## Emotional Risk (ER) score

The **ER score** is a continuous measure of your current emotional state based on what PsychAssist observes about your recent session activity. The scale runs from negative to positive:

| ER reading | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Elevated positive** | Clear-headed — conditions are favorable for disciplined execution |
| **Near zero** | Neutral — stay attentive |
| **Below –1.5** | Elevated risk — impulse or revenge trade likelihood is statistically higher |

PsychAssist updates the ER score in real time. It factors in session-level signals including your infraction count and emotional control ratings from the Session Journal.

### ER Meter

A small **ER indicator** is visible in the main Fintheon interface at all times. Glance at it the same way you'd glance at a heat meter — it tells you the current temperature before you act, not after.

<Note>
  The ER score is advisory. A low score is a prompt to slow down, not a trading halt. You remain in full control of every decision.
</Note>

## Morning Routine Gate

Each trading morning, PsychAssist may present a **Morning Routine Gate** before you can access the full Consilium workspace. The gate is a short pre-market calibration checklist:

1. Open Fintheon — read the tape, see the briefing
2. Check NarrativeFlow — Consilium analyst memos
3. Complete top-down analysis
4. Confirm you are mentally calibrated and ready to trade

Work through each step and check it off. Once all four are marked, click **Routine Complete** to proceed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the tape">
    Open Fintheon and review overnight futures moves and the current MDB brief before looking at any individual instrument.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check NarrativeFlow">
    Scan the catalyst cards in NarrativeFlow. Identify the two or three narratives most likely to affect your instruments today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Top-down analysis">
    Work from macro to micro: index regime → sector → instrument. Confirm your read on directional bias and key levels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mental check-in">
    Honestly assess whether you are focused and even-keeled. If you are carrying stress from outside the market, note it. Entering the session aware of emotional noise is the point of the gate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The Morning Routine Gate includes a **Skip (not recommended)** option. Using it bypasses the gate but the skip is logged. Frequent skips will appear in your session journal data and may correlate with elevated ER readings later in those sessions.
</Warning>

## Hot Hand Banner

The **Hot Hand Banner** appears at the top of the Consilium workspace when PsychAssist detects a streak of significant wins that statistically elevates overconfidence risk.

When the banner is active it shows:
- How many hours remain in the 48-hour watch window
- The win amount that triggered the flag

The banner text reads: *"Verify thesis quality before entry. Standard sizing only."* That is the practical instruction — confirm your analysis is sound before adding size, and resist the pull to deviate from your normal risk parameters just because recent trades went well.

<Accordion title="What triggers the Hot Hand flag?">
  A single large win triggers a 48-hour overconfidence watch. The banner stays visible until the window expires or you explicitly dismiss it. The flag is based on the statistical pattern that a sequence of wins increases the probability of an outsized risk-on decision, regardless of whether the wins were deserved.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does the Hot Hand Banner affect my position limits?">
  No. PsychAssist does not interact with your order management or position limits. The banner is informational only. Sizing and risk decisions remain entirely yours.
</Accordion>

## How PsychAssist connects to Voice mode

If your ER score drops below the threshold or you log multiple infractions in a short window, PsychAssist may trigger an intervention via [Voice mode](/trading/voice). Harper will speak a brief check-in prompt to acknowledge what the data is showing. This only fires when voice is enabled and after a 10-minute cooldown between interventions — it will not interrupt you repeatedly.

## PsychAssist scores in your journal

Your session-level self-scores (Discipline Score and Emotional Control) feed back into PsychAssist's view of your performance over time. The ER score visible on each [Day History Card](/trading/journal#day-history-cards) in the Trading Journal is the snapshot from that session. Reviewing these over a 30-day window is one of the most effective ways to identify the market conditions or times of day when your emotional discipline tends to slip.

<Tip>
  After a streak of high-ER sessions, look at the Blindspots row in the Performance Dashboard. The two views are designed to be used together — ER tells you when it's happening, Blindspots tells you what the specific pattern is.
</Tip>
