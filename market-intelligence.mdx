# Market intelligence

Fintheon combines market data, economic intelligence, source discovery, and instrument context so the trader can inspect a change with its provenance.

## Included surfaces

- Economic calendar, prior prints, surprises, earnings, history, and timezone-aware event handling.
- Futures, options, equities sentiment, volatility, price action, GEX, rates, regime context, and market scan.
- Global search, source accounts, commentators, catalysts, articles, and research discovery.
- Instrument-aware context with clear freshness and source labels.

Use this surface to inspect the evidence behind a market condition before carrying it into RiskFlow, NarrativeFlow, Desk, or a proposal.
